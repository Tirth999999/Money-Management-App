Take total control of your personal finances with My Money Manager, your intelligent, private financial companion featuring the Jarvis AI voice assistant. Built natively for Android with modern Material 3 and Jetpack Compose, this app combines the clean, intuitive simplicity of traditional expense tracking with powerful on-device automation.

Never forget to record a transaction again.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS & CORE CAPABILITIES:

1. AUTOMATIC HDFC BANK SMS DETECTION
• Instantly detects incoming debit and credit transaction SMS messages from HDFC Bank.
• Automatically parses merchant/recipient, transaction amount, reference number, and account mode.
• Converts messages into Pending Drafts so you remain in complete control—approve, edit, or reject before anything touches your real account balance.
• Fully handles UPI debits, credit card spends, netbanking transfers, and salary credits.

2. JARVIS HANDS-FREE VOICE ASSISTANT
• Add transactions effortlessly by speaking naturally in English, Hindi, or Gujarati (e.g. "add 100 cash expense", "paid 500 online for dinner", or "pachas rupiya kharch").
• Continuous Voice-to-Voice Conversation: If any mandatory information is missing, Jarvis speaks back to ask clarification, automatically opens the mic, and lets you answer hands-free.
• Customizable Bot Persona: Choose between deep tech male, classic male, crisp female, British tech, or device system voices. Adjust tone pitch and speech rate to your exact preference.
• Quick Speak Launcher Shortcut: 1-tap voice entry right from your Android home screen without having to navigate through app menus.

3. MULTI-ACCOUNT & FUND MANAGEMENT
• Pre-configured with Online (HDFC) and Cash accounts, with unlimited support for custom fund buckets (Savings, Emergency Fund, Investments, etc.).
• Complete Account Editing: Edit account names, modify opening/initial balances, customize icons and colors, or delete accounts whenever needed.
• Internal Fund Transfers: Seamlessly transfer money between accounts with exact ledger tracking without distorting your income or expense analytics.
• Exact Integer Arithmetic: All currency calculations are stored and computed in paise to eliminate floating-point rounding errors.

4. PENDING DRAFTS QUEUE & COMPLETE EDITING
• Review every detected transaction before it updates your account balance.
• Full draft editing: modify amount, income/expense type, account, suggested category, or description with "Save Draft" or "Save & Approve" actions.
• Dismiss or reject duplicate and non-ledger transactions cleanly.

5. LENDING, BORROWING & DEBT LEDGER
• Track money lent to friends and money borrowed from contacts.
• View real-time net receivables and liabilities.
• Record full or partial repayments with automatic account balance adjustments.

6. DAILY REVIEW REMINDERS AT 9 PM
• Automated daily reminder at 9:00 PM if unreviewed pending drafts exist.
• High-priority Android 14+ compatible notifications with quick tap-to-review action.
• Instant in-app notification test button to verify system permissions immediately.

7. DEEP FINANCIAL ANALYSIS & CHARTS
• Interactive breakdown of monthly income vs. expenses, net cash flow, and daily spending velocity.
• Category-wise expense distribution and account balance contributions.
• Search and filter transactions by date, time period, account, or type.

8. 100% OFFLINE PRIVACY GUARANTEE
• Zero third-party cloud trackers, zero external database servers, zero third-party bank APIs.
• All transaction records, contact names, and SMS history remain strictly on your device inside an encrypted SQLite Room database.
• Works flawlessly with or without an active internet connection.
